The Wheel of the Year

The old year turns through eight great points: Yule, Disting, Ostara, Walpurgis, Midsummer, Freyfaxi, Winter Finding, and Winter Nights. These are the lodge's seasonal dispatches, oldest first, so they can be read as the year actually moves. New ones are added as each turning comes around.
