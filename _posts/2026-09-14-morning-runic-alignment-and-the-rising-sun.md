## The Laying of the Morning Staves

At 8:30 AM on Monday morning, the mountain air is crisp at 54°F under a steady northwest breeze. 

Before diving into the week's complex technical and mechanical responsibilities, the practitioner lays out the morning staves—not as fatalistic prediction, but as a deliberate calibration of intention and focus.

## Four Vectors of Action

1. **ᚱ Raidho (The Wheel & Mechanical Alignment):**  
   Raidho represents vehicles, transit, and returning mechanical systems to their rightful motion. Whether rebuilding a transfer case linkage or tuning a network bridge, Raidho demands that force translate into true, unhindered forward movement.
2. **ᚦ Thurisaz (The Hammer & Overcoming Resistance):**  
   The focused force of Thor’s hammer breaking through stubborn knots, rusted threads, and seized steel. It is the patience to apply leverage, heat, and penetrating focus to dissolve friction without shearing the bolt.
3. **ᛃ Jera (Steady Momentum & Natural Cycle):**  
   The reminder that Monday does not require panic. Systems are built step by step; tasks are resolved one commit at a time. Trust the cadence of steady effort.
4. **ᛉ Algiz (The Protective Ward):**  
   Holding the domestic sanctuary safe and peaceful while the day's external labors are pursued.
