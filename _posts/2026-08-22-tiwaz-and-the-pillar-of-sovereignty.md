## The Upward Spear and the Steady Star

In the Elder Futhark, the rune **Tiwaz (ᛏ)** is shaped like an upward-pointing spear or the central pillar upholding the sky. Named for **Tyr**, the ancient Germanic and Norse sky god of cosmic order, justice, and honorable self-sacrifice, Tiwaz represents the unshakeable moral compass that maintains stability in a world of entropic drift.

On an empirical homestead, Tiwaz is not an aggressive warrior rune; it is the **pillar of sovereignty, discipline, and promises kept**.

---

### 1. 🛡️ Establishing Sovereign Order Over Your Domain
Entropy is the natural tendency of all physical systems to degrade into disorder—tools rust, weeds overtake beds, and digital noise invades the mind. 

Tiwaz is the conscious application of willpower to restore order. It governs the establishment of firm, healthy boundaries: shutting down external work chatter when the day is done, silencing unnecessary digital notifications, and defending the peace of the domestic hearth (*Innangarð*). It teaches that true freedom is impossible without boundary discipline.

---

### 2. ⚖️ The Hand in the Wolf's Mouth: The Weight of Responsibility
In the most famous myth of the Norse corpus, Tyr places his right hand inside the jaws of the monstrous wolf Fenrir as a pledge of good faith, knowingly sacrificing his own limb so the gods could bind chaos and preserve Asgard.

Tiwaz embodies the principle that **sovereignty requires sacrifice**:
* The willingness to endure discomfort for long-term resilience.
* The discipline of daily homestead chores—hauling water in freezing weather, turning heavy compost, and maintaining mechanical equipment before it breaks.
* Honoring every commitment made to family, land, and community.

---

### 3. 🌟 The Pole Star: The Fixed Point in Changing Winds
In early runic poetry, Tiwaz is directly identified with the **North Star (Polaris)**:
> *"Tir is a star, which princes well hold faith to / Ever on its course above the clouds of night, it never fails."*

While barometric pressure systems cycle and mountain weather shifts violently from humid heat to autumn frost, Polaris remains steady. Tiwaz reminds the practitioner to remain emotionally and spiritually unshakeable, rooted in clear purpose regardless of external chaos.

---

*“Honor is the axle around which the homestead turns; without the spear of truth, the hearth cannot endure.”*
