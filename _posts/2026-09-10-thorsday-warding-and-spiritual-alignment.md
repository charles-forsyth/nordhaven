Today on the Omstead ridge, the morning sun breaks through crisp autumn air at 74°F while the laying flock free-ranges across the back grass. September brings the quiet turning of the season—the second harvest, the dropping of the sap, and the steady, unyielding strength of **Þórsdagr (Thor’s Day)**.

---

### 🌩️ The Spiritual Insight: Strength on the Anvil

While Wednesday belongs to Odin’s search for hidden knowledge and visionary insight, Thursday belongs to **Thor**—the Red-Bearded Defender of Midgard. Thor represents the resilience of the homesteader: the strength to stand firm against storms, administrative friction, and the relentless ticking of life's demands.

When you watch pastured hens scratching in the grass or look out over a well-guarded perimeter, you are witnessing Thor's peace. It is the peace earned through labor, boundary upkeep, and absolute steadfastness.

---

### 🛡️ The Rice & Iron Warding for the Homestead

*Spoken quietly over the threshold of the lodge:*

> **"By Hammer of North and Stone of Ground,  
> Let iron wall and shield go round.  
> 
> Guard the ridge and guard the gate,  
> Keep all discord far from fate.  
> Ward the roof and ward the floor,  
> Bar the sickness from the door.  
> 
> Protect the man, protect the wife,  
> Guard the creatures, guard the life.  
> By Thunder’s might and Earth’s deep grace,  
> Peace and power hold this place.  
> 
> So it is spoken. So it is warded. Hail Þórr!"**

### ☀️ Daily Blessing for the Ridge

May the sun stay warm upon the back grass, may the boundaries of home remain absolute and secure, and may the rest of this Thursday bring absolute clarity, steady rhythm, and quiet evening rest. 

*— Chuck & Laylla, Omstead Lodge*
