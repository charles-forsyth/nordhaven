## The Waxing Light of the Sturgeon Moon

Tonight is governed by **ᛃ Jera** (the rune of the seasonal wheel and the earned fruit) paired with **ᛉ Algiz** (the protective shield).

Think of the biological alignment anchored across the homestead:
* The living soil, recently drenched with active microbial ferments, drinks deeply in the cool dark.
* The squashes, tomatoes, and late cabbages stand firm in their raised smart containers.
* The flock roosts peacefully in the cedar-framed coop.
* The home is latched against the rising mountain winds.

When you walk the boundary stones under an 88% moon, the rhythm of the year becomes visceral. Summer’s raw, explosive growth is yielding to autumn’s dense, quiet maturation.
