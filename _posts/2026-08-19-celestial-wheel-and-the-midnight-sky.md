## Máni's Chariot and Subterranean Pull

In Old Norse lore, the moon is not a lifeless rock; it is **Máni**, who drives his luminous chariot across the heavens, navigating the star paths while pursued by the cosmic wolf *Hati*.

Tonight, the moon rides at a **Waxing Crescent of 32.8% illumination**. 

In traditional planting lore and animist agronomy, the waxing crescent exerts a gentle, upward transpirational pull upon subterranean fluids. It is the prime astrological window for two complementary acts of cultivation:
1. **Brewing Living Biology:** Actively aerating organic microbial teas. The oxygenated water column, kept warm at 72°F by submersible elements, supports exponential aerobic bacterial division under the waxing light.
2. **Root Crop Emergence:** Sowing cold-hardy autumn brassicas (rutabagas, radishes, and winter greens) in breathable containers, where the moisture gradient draws the embryonic taproot downward while the seed leaf unfurls toward the stars.

This is the manifestation of **ᛃ Jera**—the rune of the seasonal cycle. You do not force nature to yield; you align your labor with the turning of the wheel.
