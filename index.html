The Ridge and the Wire

Nordhaven is a working homestead and an empirical laboratory: a pastured flock, fabric-pot gardens fed on living teas, a mesh of sensors on the ridge, an old truck and a small camper, a woodstove, a ridge cat who keeps his own hours, and the old Norse ways kept alongside the measurements. It is written under the lodge's name, on purpose: the place and the practice are the point.
