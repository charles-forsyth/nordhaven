How this manual is kept

The chronicle is dated: each dispatch is one day, one lesson, written down and left as it was. The manual is the other half. These pages are tended and revised as the practice changes, so they always describe how things are done now. When a dispatch teaches something lasting, the lesson moves here.

Nothing here is a prescription for your land or your machines. It is one lodge's practice, written plainly so it can be checked, borrowed or argued with.
