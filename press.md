## 📚 Featured Publication

### **Building a Farmstead for Science**
*Applied Agronomy, Spatial Mesoclimates, and Closed-Loop Homestead Engineering*  
**Author:** Chuck Forsyth  
**Imprint:** Omstead Press &bull; September 2026  
**Format:** 20 Chapters &bull; Comprehensive Field Treatise  

#### Abstract
Moving beyond the romanticized folklore of self-sufficiency, *Building a Farmstead for Science* establishes a rigorous empirical framework for modern homesteading. Grounded in soil chemistry, thermodynamics, avian epidemiology, and autonomous IoT telemetry, this master volume outlines the exact protocols, mathematical models, and engineering blueprints required to operate a resilient, closed-loop homestead in the Northern Appalachian plateau.
