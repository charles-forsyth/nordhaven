A finding: OmNet's GDD runs high

OmNet's nightly GDD job takes the day's min and max from the three Blink camera housings. In afternoon sun those read up to 115 °F, so the daily "high" is inflated. Bars: daily GDD (base 50 °F) from OmNet vs from Google's air temperature for the same day. Hover for the min/max each one used.
