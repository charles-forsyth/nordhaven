Google Weather Insights: first tests

Three interactive looks at Google's historical hourly weather in BigQuery, subscribed Oct 20, 2026 through the Maps Platform testing program. Each page is a single self-contained file.

1

One hot day, hour by hour

The whole lower 48 for 24 hours on July 15, 2026. Temperature vs wet-bulb: dry desert heat vs dangerous humid heat.

 2

Inland heat: UC Riverside vs the coast

Southern California at the native ~4.6 km grid. UCR peaked about 14 °C hotter than Santa Monica that afternoon.

 3

Omstead's 2026 season vs OmNet

Frost dates, growing degree days and rain for Center Ridge, checked hour by hour against OmNet's own records. Found a GDD inflation in OmNet.
Dataset
ucr-research-computing · weather_insights___us.weather_history_us (also ___gb)
Coverage
~4.6 km grid, hourly, Jan 2024 to about a day ago, 22 fields
Query cost
All queries for these pages billed 27 GB together (about $0.17 at list price). Dry-run estimates are upper bounds and ran ~10× high; the 8-month Omstead pull was estimated at 226 GB and billed 23 GB.
Status
Testing program; terms for sharing with researchers not yet confirmed. Do not redistribute.
